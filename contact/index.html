Do I need to know which service I need?

No. Describe the business challenge and what you have already tried. The first recommendation should address the constraint—not force the situation into a service package.

Should I include my advertising budget?

You can share an approximate range during the first conversation if paid media is relevant. It helps determine whether the proposed approach is commercially sensible.

Can I enquire if my business is still small?

Yes. Fit depends more on the problem, expectations and readiness to act than on company size. A smaller, focused project can often be the right place to begin.
