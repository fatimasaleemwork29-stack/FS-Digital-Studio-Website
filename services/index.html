Services

The right work starts with the right problem.

FS Digital Studio connects strategy, campaigns, digital experiences and follow-up. Each engagement is shaped around the constraint in your customer journey—not a generic package.

StrategyMeta AdsWebsitesLead generationConversion
01
Best for

Businesses doing “a bit of everything” without a clear priority or connected plan.

Digital marketing strategy

Decide what matters before adding more marketing.

A strategic foundation clarifies who you need to reach, why they should care, where to reach them and what should happen next.

Business problem

Channels and content are active, but decisions are reactive and performance is difficult to interpret.

Strategy

Map the audience, offer, message, acquisition channels and conversion path around commercial priorities.

Deliverables

Strategic audit, customer-journey map, channel roles, campaign priorities and practical action plan.

Intended outcome

A clearer direction for where to invest attention, time and budget—and what to stop doing.

02
Best for

Brands ready to move beyond boosted posts and build campaigns around audience intent.

Meta Ads

Campaigns with a role in the funnel—not isolated promotions.

Meta advertising works best when targeting, creative, landing experience and follow-up support the same customer decision.

Business problem

Budget is spent on boosted posts or campaigns without clear objectives, funnel stages or useful learning.

Strategy

Align campaign objectives, audience intent, creative angles and retargeting with the conversion journey.

Deliverables

Account and funnel review, campaign structure, audience plan, creative direction and measurement framework.

Intended outcome

A more deliberate path from paid attention to enquiry or purchase, with clearer signals for optimization.

03
Best for

Businesses whose website does not yet match the quality of their offer or support conversion.

Website design & development

A digital experience that earns attention and makes action easier.

A website should explain the offer, reduce uncertainty and guide the right visitor toward a useful next step—across every screen size.

Business problem

The current site feels unclear, dated, difficult on mobile or disconnected from advertising and lead generation.

Strategy

Structure information around visitor intent, decision friction, trust and the primary conversion path.

Deliverables

Information architecture, conversion copy, responsive UX/UI, development and essential SEO setup.

Intended outcome

A clearer, more credible digital presence that supports campaigns and qualified customer action.

04
Best for

Service businesses and growing brands that need a more intentional enquiry pipeline.

Lead generation

Create a useful reason to respond—and a clear way to do it.

Lead generation connects the offer, audience, message, landing experience and follow-up into a practical acquisition pathway.

Business problem

Traffic or outreach generates interest, but enquiries are inconsistent, low-context or difficult to qualify.

Strategy

Define the offer, lead pathway, qualification points and handoff between campaign and conversation.

Deliverables

Offer and message development, landing-page plan, form structure, outreach or campaign pathway and tracking plan.

Intended outcome

A more structured flow of relevant conversations, with better context for follow-up and evaluation.

05
Best for

Businesses receiving visits or enquiries but losing momentum between interest and action.

Conversion & retargeting

Improve what happens after the first click.

Conversion thinking identifies friction across the landing experience, call to action, form, follow-up and return journey.

Business problem

People visit, browse or engage, but too few take the next step—or they disappear after showing interest.

Strategy

Review behaviour and messaging, reduce friction, strengthen the next step and create a responsible retargeting plan.

Deliverables

Conversion-path review, landing recommendations, CTA and form improvements, retargeting map and testing priorities.

Intended outcome

A clearer journey that gives interested visitors fewer reasons to leave and more relevant reasons to return.

Not sure where to begin?

Start with the constraint, not the service list.
